Privacy and analytics.

This page explains the optional analytics used on this portfolio and the separate request that retrieves public GitHub repositories.

Who is responsible

Data controller: Maximiliano Sá

Contact: max.phillips.sa@gmail.com

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure visits and understand how people use the portfolio. Analytics is optional. The legal basis used for this analytics processing is consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). The analytics tag and related storage are activated only after you select Accept analytics.

You can select Reject analytics in the banner. When you reject, the GA4 tag is not loaded and this site sends no Google Analytics events. You can reopen your choice at any time with Cookie preferences in the footer. If you withdraw consent, this site updates its consent state, stops further analytics collection, and removes accessible GA cookies where possible. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before it was withdrawn. Data already sent to Google cannot be recalled by this site.

After consent, measurements sent to Google Analytics may include page URLs and titles, browser and device information, interactions with the site, and online identifiers such as GA4 cookies. Google processes these measurements to provide its analytics service. Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. For visitors in the European Union, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google says IP addresses are used to derive approximate geographic information and are discarded before measurement data is logged. Google states that these IP addresses are not logged, accessible, or used for additional purposes in this context. See Google's Analytics data safeguards and EU, UK and Switzerland data and privacy information.

Google states that information may be processed on servers outside the country where it was collected. Google describes its transfer frameworks, including adequacy decisions and contractual safeguards, in its data transfer information.

Retention

The GA4 property is configured for 2 months of event-level data and 14 months of user-level data. The user-level retention period resets when new activity occurs. These settings apply to user- and event-level data used by certain features and reports; they do not mean all standard aggregated reports are deleted after these periods. See Google's data retention documentation.

Your choices and rights

You may request applicable data protection rights, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and, where applicable, data portability. Contact the data controller using the email above. You may also complain to the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD). The availability of particular rights depends on the circumstances and applicable law.

GitHub repositories

This portfolio retrieves public repository information from GitHub's API at api.github.com to display details about my software projects. This request is separate from Google Analytics and may occur even if you reject analytics consent. GitHub may receive your IP address and technical information associated with the API request. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. GitHub Pages also hosts this website.
